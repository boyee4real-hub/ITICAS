METHODOLOGY & SCIENTIFIC CAUTION

How these spatial results should be interpreted

Run the analysis to see methodology notes.

Raw GPS point density alone is not treated as congestion. Inferential hot/cold-spot labels are also withheld when a route has less than 20% temporal coverage or fewer than 24 evidence-bearing hours. ITICAS weights spatial intensity by a traffic-performance variable such as congestion index, delay or reliability. This avoids confusing sampling density with traffic severity.

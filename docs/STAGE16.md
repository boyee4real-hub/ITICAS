# ITICAS Stage 16 — GNSS + GIS Traffic Research Workbench

This stage converts ITICAS from a traffic-intelligence application into a research workflow platform for studies such as traffic-congestion hotspot mapping using GNSS and GIS.

Core additions:
- Browser/mobile high-accuracy GNSS survey sessions and track-point storage.
- Research-objective coverage matrix.
- Provider road-segment geometry and corridor probe generation.
- GIS exchange: GeoJSON, KML, GPX and chainage CSV.
- Extensive GNSS–GIS research package combining raw data, analytical tables, publication reports, decision atlas and reproducibility manifest.
- Explicit evidence gates: unsupported traffic volume/density/direction/queue/hotspot significance is not fabricated.
